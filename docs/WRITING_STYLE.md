# Blog writing style

Casual and clear, like explaining something to a friend who codes. Mostly
plain, with the occasional dry line that comes from the situation.

## Do

- Write in first person about what actually happened. Specific numbers and
  details (five sessions, twenty minutes, 800ms) over general claims.
- Keep the humor dry and about the situation ("built at a desk under normal
  lighting, which is a lot less atmospheric"). One or two per post is plenty.
- Use plain section headings that label the content: "The first version",
  "How it's set up". Short posts can skip them.
- End when the story is done. A personal note is fine; advice to the reader
  is not.
- Titles say what the post is about: "Building ghosts-playground", not a
  slogan.

## Avoid

- Self-deprecating jokes.
- Mottos as headings ("The loop is the product").
- "It's not X, it's Y" and "X isn't just A. It's B."
- Wise one-liners closing a paragraph or section.
- Closing calls to action ("Next time you…", "give it an afternoon").
- Bold labels in front of paragraphs, groups of three, em dashes.
- Words like "compounds", "signal", "the move", "unlock", "leverage".
